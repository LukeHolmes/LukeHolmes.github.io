What the platform covers

Training for the overlap between operations, validation, and review.

GXPLearn sits in a practical lane: it uses simulation-style exercises and guided modules to build familiarity with the language, evidence, and decision points that appear around regulated manufacturing systems.

The focus is not just on one discipline. The platform bridges system literacy, validation context, documentation review, and the day-to-day judgement calls that sit around alarms, deviations, and evidence packages.

  • DeltaV-style system literacy without presenting itself as official vendor training
  • ISA-88 recipe structure, HMI and alarms, historian and trend interpretation, and PID concepts
  • Audit trails, data integrity, CSV, CSA, deviations, change control, and review readiness
  • Batch-record review and validation package review in guided scenarios
Independent by design Conservative positioning matters here.

This project page intentionally describes GXPLearn as an independent learning platform in public beta. It does not claim regulator approval, official certification, or official DeltaV / Emerson status.

Learning pathways

Foundation layer Modules 01-03 Free

Entry modules focused on architecture, ISA-88 recipe thinking, and validation context. Designed as a low-friction start for new learners.

  • Account required, no payment
  • Introduces the platform language and mental models
  • Good fit for first exposure to regulated-system context
Professional curriculum Modules 04-22 + capstone EUR 99 one-time

Broader simulation and review track that extends into control context, evidence interpretation, and validation workflows.

  • About 23 guided modules in total across the product
  • Includes the Validation Digital Twin capstone workspace
  • Targets individual learners building regulated-industry fluency
Team / Site Internal training licence EUR 3,500 per year

Team-facing offer aimed at onboarding and internal capability building for one company or site.

  • Invoice-based purchasing
  • Unlimited internal use within one company or site
  • Founder-led support and pilot options on the live site

Portfolio angle

From CSASim to GXPLearn

A prototype becoming a product.

The earlier CSASim portfolio entry captured the original idea: browser-native, simulation-flavoured learning around CSV, CSA, automation context, and validation workflows.

GXPLearn is the more complete expression of that work. It keeps the simulation-first posture, but now wraps it in a clearer curriculum, sharper commercial packaging, and a more explicit split between free foundations, paid professional learning, and team adoption.

For the portfolio, that makes GXPLearn the right front-door project name, with CSASim retained only as the concept it grew out of.

Why feature it It connects validation domain knowledge to product execution.

GXPLearn is a strong portfolio lead because it combines subject-matter depth, education design, and browser delivery in one product story.

  • Domain-specific training problem, not a generic demo
  • Product packaging with visible pricing and pathways
  • Clear evolution from prototype to independent platform
